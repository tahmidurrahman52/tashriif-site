01 · The rule

A machine should be rented only after all our own machines are in use

Expected practice

Each floor uses its own machines first, with a small standby for breakdown. Only the gap above that is rented — and every rented machine is running and accounted for.
≠

What the audit shows

Every floor already has enough own machines for its operators, yet 92 rented machines sit on the floors and ~203 own machines are not running. Another 20 billed machines are not on any floor.
Scope: the 6 machine types on rent — SNLS, Overlock, Flatlock, Bartack, Hole, FOA. EKL 2nd Floor, EKL 5th Floor and Bally Cotton.

06 · Control gaps found

Why this was not visible earlier

🧾

Rent bills not matched to the floor

19 machines are billed but cannot be found; 1 returned machine is still billed; 1 serial is listed twice.

🔧

No status for own machines

~203 own machines are not running, with no record of whether they are broken, in store or idle in line.

✅

No own-stock check before renting

Rentals rose from July (87 of 112) while each floor already had enough own machines.

⏳

Long rentals not reviewed

25 machines on rent for over 6 months, 14 for over a year (oldest 544 days).

07 · For management discussion

Points to decide

How should the 19 "Can't Find In Floor" machines be settled with the vendors — and from when should billing stop?
Should the 10 idle rented machines and the 1 returned machine be taken off the bill now?
Should a status check of all own machines (running / idle / breakdown / store) be done on every floor?
Should own idle machines be moved into lines before any rented machine is kept or added?
Who approves a new rental, and should it require a floor-wise own-stock check?
What standby allowance should each floor follow — 5% or another figure?
