Manpower · Costing

Helper Reduction & Piece-Rate Analysis

A contractual-operator knit-dress factory ran with a 42–45% helper share. I built the case to cut helpers through small devices and operator-side task changes, with a piece-rate offer to keep operators on side.

13lines in scope
3improvement cases
1 / linehelper removed per case
The three cases
  • Shoulder join: a trimming device on the overlock removes the trimming helper on all 13 lines.
  • Back tape piping: operator cuts the piping, removing one helper per line on 9 lines.
  • Sleeve join: operator folds the body, removing one helper per line on 13 lines.
Deliverable
  • Formula-driven workbook for MD, Chairman and Director: executive summary, inputs, one sheet per case, piece-rate sensitivity, risk and manpower view.
  • Operator incentive range modelled through a piece-rate increase to manage contractor resistance.

Strategy · Business case

IE Department Proposal

A proposal to build a dedicated IE team for 1,300 sewing machines across 48 lines, with cutting and finishing in scope, where no time study, line balancing or layout work was being done.

55.5% → 65%efficiency target
$1.8M+yearly value of the gap
3hiring phases
The case
  • Mapped the current workload: daily SMV for 20–25 styles, input-ensure data chasing 3–4 hours a day, manual line checks.
  • Valued the efficiency gap using ERP capacity and CM figures.
  • Phased recruitment: one experienced IE per floor, the rest trained freshers.
  • Delivered as a PowerPoint deck and a Word proposal for the MD.
